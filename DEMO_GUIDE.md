# Demo guide (3 minutes)

1. **Citizen** — sign in as `ramesh.kumar@gov.in`. Show the "My applications" tab: requests from different departments in one place with one timeline.
2. **Search** — type `mere area mein paani nahi aa raha`, click **Find the right department**. OneGov picks Water Board. Point out "Auto-filled from your profile" (nothing typed again) and submit. Expand "What was sent to the department's system" to show the connector message.
3. **Duplicate** — search `income certificate` and submit: OneGov blocks the duplicate request (Ramesh already has one).
4. **Consent** — sign in as `pooja@example.in` (password `Citizen@2026`), search `caste certificate`, submit without ticking consent (blocked), then tick and submit. Open *Profile & consent* to revoke.
5. **Officer** — sign out, choose *Department officer → Water Board*, sign in. Show requests, SLA overdue count, then **AI groups**: similar water complaints in Ward 7 are grouped. Click **Advance all** or **Resolve all**.
6. **Back to citizen** — the status and notification have changed everywhere.
7. **Admin** — `admin@gov.in`. Show Monitoring (SLA compliance, duplicates blocked, fields auto-filled), **Connectors** (tick "Simulate outage" for Water Board, submit a water complaint as a citizen, see it queued, then **Retry**), Workflow config (change an SLA), Audit log, Data quality.

Sentence to close with: *"No department system is replaced. OneGov connects them."*
